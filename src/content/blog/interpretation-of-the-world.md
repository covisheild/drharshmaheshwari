## 2. Reality is neutral, but our interpretation shapes our experience of it

Reality is neutral, but our interpretation of reality shapes our emotional experience of it. Two people can experience the same situation and feel completely differently because they interpret it differently.

Our interpretation influences our feelings, and those feelings, in turn, influence our subsequent actions. This creates a chain:

> **Our interpretation → Our feelings → Our actions → Our future experiences.**

We may not always control what happens to us, but we have some influence over how we interpret it. So, choose your interpretation wisely.

## 3. The world is a better place than it used to be, especially when it comes to careers

I believe the world today offers far more opportunities than it did decades or centuries ago. This is particularly true from a career perspective.

In India, too, I feel there has been greater recognition of merit and a greater celebration of meritocracy than there was 20, 30, or 50 years ago. Opportunities are not equally accessible to everyone, and meritocracy is far from perfect. Nevertheless, the possibilities available today are worth recognizing.

We should not judge the entire world by the limitations we encounter in our immediate surroundings or by the experiences of previous generations.
