finding: "heart.s4"
title: "S4 (fourth heart sound)"
listen: "A low extra sound just before S1, at the very end of diastole: \"da-lub-dub\", the rhythm of \"Ten-nes-see\"."
means: "The atrium pushing blood into a stiff ventricle, as in hypertension, left ventricular hypertrophy or ischaemia. It cannot occur in atrial fibrillation."
references:
  - text: "Bickley LS, Szilagyi PG, Hoffman RM, Soriano RP. Bates' Guide to Physical Examination and History Taking. 13th ed. Philadelphia: Wolters Kluwer; 2021. Cardiovascular system chapter."
  - text: "Marcus GM, Gerber IL, McKeown BH, et al. Association between phonocardiographic third and fourth heart sounds and objective measures of left ventricular function. JAMA. 2005;293(18):2238-44."
    url: "https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.293.18.2238"
