# Hindi (hi): native rewrite check, 13 Oct 2026

## Native rewrite (13 Oct 2026)

`src/i18n/hi.json` (383 changed strings) and the three Hindi articles (`learn/bmi-cut-offs-for-indians`,
`learn/waist-to-height-ratio`, `blog/interpretation-of-the-world`) were rewritten to read as everyday Hindi.
This check had two steps. First, a blind back-translation: only the Hindi was read, and each line was given a naturalness score N from 1 to 5.
Second, each line was compared with the English and graded for changes of meaning.

### Result

- Grades: **CRITICAL 0**, **MODERATE 5** (all fixed), **MINOR 10** (2 fixed, 8 left because they are harmless).
- Naturalness of the 383 changed strings as found: mean **N = 4.77**. 305 lines scored 5, 70 scored 4, 7 scored 3 and 1 scored 2.
  After the fixes the estimate is **about 4.82**: every line at 3 or below was rewritten and is now 4–5.
- Safety lines read correctly, with the right numbers, direction and urgency: low sugar [280], very high BP [270], high sugar [78], sleepy
  driving [31], medicines [87], pregnancy [144]. In [78] and [280], drowsy is translated as उनींदा/उनींदापन, without "बहुत" (very).
- `npm run i18n:check -- hi`: no errors. The articles were re-stamped with `npm run i18n:status -- --stamp`.

### Voice notes (from the native pass style sheet)

- **Reader:** a family in Lucknow, Raipur or Delhi.
- **Writer:** their family doctor.
- **Address:** always आप. Verbs use the polite imperative (नापें, भरें, देख लें, करवाएँ). Never तुम.
- **Register:** spoken Hindustani. Use नाप, सेहत, फ़ायदा, मौक़ा, हिसाब, अंदाज़ा, पक्का पता. Avoid textbook words such as गणना, अनुमान लगाना, निदान (alone), उपयोग, भुगतान.
- **Loanwords** are kept as people say them: सेव, शेयर, लिंक, ऐप, डिवाइस, स्टोरेज, रीडिंग, रेंज, BP, शुगर. Never सहेजें.
- **Particles** make it sound written in Hindi: भी, ही, तो, बस. For "not sent or stored", use "न कहीं भेजा जाता है, न कहीं सेव होता है". For "until", use "जब तक ... न करें".
- **Fixed clinical words:**
  - energetic = ऊर्जावान (मध्यम से ज़ोरदार)
  - vigorous = ज़ोरदार
  - severe = गंभीर
  - drowsy = उनींदा, confused = भ्रम (कन्फ़्यूज़न), with no "बहुत"
  - teaspoon = छोटे चम्मच (टीस्पून)
  - strapped in = पट्टे (बेल्ट) में बँधा
  - adult = वयस्क
  - sex = लिंग
  - contribution = योगदान/सहयोग, never दान
- **Risk tiers stay distinct:** कम जोखिम / बढ़ा हुआ जोखिम / उच्च जोखिम.
- **Kept exactly as in English:** numbers, units, placeholders, tags, hrefs, every "not"/"never", and the hedges (शायद, हो सकता है, लगभग, आम तौर पर, औसतन).
