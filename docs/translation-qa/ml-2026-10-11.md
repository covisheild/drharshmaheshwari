# Malayalam QA, 11 Oct 2026 (meaning comparison, pipeline step 4b)

Checked: the whole dictionary `src/i18n/ml.json` (953 strings) and the three texts
(`learn/bmi-cut-offs-for-indians`, `learn/waist-to-height-ratio`, `blog/interpretation-of-the-world`). A blind back-translation
(made by an agent that saw only the Malayalam) was compared pair by pair with the English: every one of the 953 strings and every
line of the three texts was read. The back-translator's notes on unnatural or ambiguous Malayalam were checked against the English
and graded with the rest. Every string containing a placeholder was also checked for a case ending joined to the placeholder.
Steps as in `docs/translation.md`.

## Not changed, and why

- By design, not findings: links to `/ml/` pages; "(ഇംഗ്ലീഷിൽ)" notes on links to English-only pages and "(the account pages are in
  English)"; English kept inside `<span lang="en">`; references, dates and `draft` taken from the English file.
- "free" (സൗജന്യ) in six strings ("Everything here is free to read and use", "Free BMI ... calculator", "Free health calculators",
  "Help keep this project free" x2, "intended to remain freely accessible"): the English says free and none is on a book page; the book,
  dataset and PDF strings do not say free (the licence line says "permission to copy, share and adapt").
- "us" in "Your support helps us keep building ...": the English says "us"; the rest of the site says "I" where the English does.
- "From 5 to 18 years" in the IAP help text: the English says 18 there (5 to 17 everywhere else). An English question for the lead, not a
  translation error.
- അറിയപ്പെടുന്ന in the BMI article ("a well-known comparison"): correct there; only "known diabetes" was wrong.
- The Y-Y paradox sentence does not name the two authors: neither does the English.
- "chemically active" (waist article): the English says so.
- Minor, left: short month labels (ഏപ്രി., ഫെബ്രു., സെപ്.) on charts are understood; "fairer to very tall and very short people" (a
  calque, understandable); the blog's "far from perfect" → "not at all perfect" and "celebration" → "acceptance" (same sense);
  "under {cm}" → "{cm}-ൽ താഴെ" with the cm unit shown after it by the page (the value is a number, so the ending is right; the word order
  would need a code change).

## Open questions (decisions, not errors)

1. **Contribution = "പിന്തുണയായി നൽകുന്ന തുക"** (an amount given as support), "Make a contribution" = "തുക നൽകി പിന്തുണയ്ക്കുക" (support by
   giving an amount). സംഭാവന (donation) stays banned. A shorter compound, പിന്തുണത്തുക, is possible but less usual.
2. **Phone labels** എല്ലാവരും / ഡോക്ടർമാർ: ഡോക്ടർമാർ (106 px) is still wider than English "Doctors" (67 px); no shorter natural plural exists
   (ഡോക്ടർ, singular, 75 px, would read as "doctor").
3. **"Reversing obesity" = "കൂടിവരുന്ന അമിതവണ്ണം തിരികെ കുറച്ചുകൊണ്ടുവരിക"** (bringing rising obesity back down), the same idea as the
   other languages.
4. **"About" = എന്നെക്കുറിച്ച് ("about me")**, matching the first-person About page; ആമുഖം ("introduction") is the alternative.
