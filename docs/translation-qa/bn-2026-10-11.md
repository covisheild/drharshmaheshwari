## Open questions (decisions, not errors)

1. **"Activity" = কার্যকলাপ.** It is the standard Bengali health-writing word and does not mean toil, but it is slightly formal. Friendlier options
   (নড়াচড়া, "movement"; শরীরচর্চা, "exercise") are narrower than "activity". Not a glossary term, so it can be changed in one pass.
2. **Disclaimer = ডিসক্লেমার**, the English word, because no plain Bengali word exists; দাবিত্যাগ is the dictionary word.
3. **Energetic = জোরদার**, close to জোরালো (vigorous). Every use has "(মাঝারি থেকে জোরালো)" beside it, as in the English.
4. **"Reversing obesity"** is now "বাড়তে থাকা স্থূলতার ধারা উল্টে দেওয়া" (turning back the rising trend), matching the Hindi choice.

## Native rewrite (13 Oct 2026)

The Bengali UI strings (437 changed lines in `src/i18n/bn.json`) and the three translated articles (`learn/bmi-cut-offs-for-indians`,
`learn/waist-to-height-ratio`, `blog/interpretation-of-the-world`) were rewritten to read as natural Kolkata chalit Bangla. Checked in two
steps: a blind back-translation of every changed line and article with a naturalness grade (1-5), then a line-by-line comparison with the English.

**Result:** CRITICAL 0, MODERATE 3, MINOR about 10 (left as they were or fixed together with the naturalness rewrites). Naturalness of the
changed lines as found: mean **4.74** (329 at 5, 104 at 4, 4 at 3, none below 3). Estimate after fixes: **4.76** (the four lines at 3 and three lines at 4 were rewritten).
Thresholds, numbers, units, negations, urgency (আজই, এখনই) and hedges (সাধারণত, প্রায়, হয়তো) all match the English. "drowsy" = ঝিমিয়ে পড়া, "sleepy in the day" = দিনের বেলা ঘুম পাওয়া, "Do not drive until you have seen a doctor" kept exact.

  ("যে মিষ্টি আছে"; in Bengal মিষ্টি means milk sweets such as sandesh or rasgulla, which a family could give instead of quick sugar). Fix: "বা হাতের কাছে মিষ্টি কিছু থাকলে সেটা"
