# Odia QA, 11 Oct 2026 (pipeline step 4b)

Meaning comparison of the Odia translation (everyday Odia for a family in Bhubaneswar or Cuttack) against the English, and the fixes made.

## What was checked

- The dictionary `src/i18n/or.json`: all 953 strings, each English original read side by side with its blind back-translation.
- The three texts in `src/content/i18n/or/` (`bmi-cut-offs-for-indians`, `waist-to-height-ratio`, `interpretation-of-the-world`),
  every sentence, including the front matter (title, description, keyPoints, faq).
- The back-translator's notes on unnatural or ambiguous Odia, each checked against the English.

These came through unchanged: every number, cut-off and threshold direction (BMI 18.5 / 22.9 / 23 / 24.9 / 25 / 30, adult-equivalent 23 / 27,
WHtR 0.5 / 0.59 / 0.6, waist 90 / 80 cm and 100 / 90 cm, the 70th / 90th waist percentiles, IDRS 30 / 50 / 60, STOP-Bang 0-2 / 3-4 / 5-8, BMI 35,
neck 43 / 41 cm, sugar 70 mg/dL (3.9 mmol/L), 15 g and 15 minutes, the ICMR-NIN and WHO minutes and days, IPAQ 600 / 1500 MET-minutes,
10 / 180 minutes and 16 hours, 72.5% / 60.1% / 1 in 6 / 95 in 100, 2,350 and 300,000 adults, 165 cm / 88 cm / 0.53 / 82.5 cm, 112), every negation
("do not stop or change a medicine", "never sent", "do not drive", "do not give anything by mouth") and the hedges "may", "on average",
"less likely, not impossible". The exceptions are in the table below.

The back-translator worried that IPAQ "Low" had two words (କମ୍ and ହାଲୁକା). It does not: ହାଲୁକା is the IDRS answer "Mild" ([451], [452], [599]),
a different English word; IPAQ Low is କମ୍ everywhere. Not a finding.

## Header labels

The full labels stay "ସମସ୍ତଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ" (for everyone) and "ଡାକ୍ତରଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ" (for doctors); Odia has no shorter natural form with "for".
Two new keys for the phone switch: **"Everyone" = ସମସ୍ତେ** (literally "everyone, all people") and **"Doctors" = ଡାକ୍ତର** (literally "doctor(s)";
Odia does not mark the plural on a label).

## Open questions (decisions, not errors)

1. **ସକ୍ରିୟତା for "activity"** is clear but slightly formal; the everyday ଚଳପ୍ରଚଳ (moving about) is used only where the English says "move more".
   A native reader could say whether ଶାରୀରିକ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକଳାପ would read better on the activity check.
2. **ଜରୁରୀ ସୂଚନା (important notice) for "Disclaimer"** is plain but loses the legal sense; the alternative is the loanword ଡିସକ୍ଲେମର.
3. **"(ମଧ୍ୟମରୁ ଜୋରଦାର)" after every "energetic"** adds words to labels; it was asked for so that energetic is never taken for vigorous.
4. **"ବେଗରେ ଚାଲିବା" for brisk walking** replaces the common ଜୋରରେ ଚାଲିବା, to keep it apart from ଜୋରଦାର (vigorous).
5. **Wasted / severely wasted** are now ବହୁତ ପତଳା / ଅତି ବେଶୀ ପତଳା (very thin / extremely thin), close to "thinness" (ପତଳାପଣ) for 5-17 years;
   the two appear in different age groups (WHO under 5, IAP from 5), so they never meet on one result.
