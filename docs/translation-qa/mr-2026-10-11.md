## Minor fixes (in brief)

- "reverse obesity" (3 strings: About description, the byline, the home tagline): लठ्ठपणा उलटवणे ("overturn", a calque) → "वाढत्या लठ्ठपणाला मागे फिरवणे" ("turning back rising obesity", the same choice as Hindi). Tagline "one clear thought at a time" (literal) → "एकेका स्पष्ट कल्पनेतून" ("through one clear idea after another").
- "the diseases that follow obesity" (2 strings) had become "caused by obesity" → "लठ्ठपणासोबत येणारे आजार" ("the diseases that come with obesity").
- Grammar: "Everything on this website ..." तो → ते; the same slip in the blog post §1 (तो → ते); "Many people at this score" (या स्कोअरच्या → हा स्कोअर असलेल्या); "This person has agreed that I keep ..." (मी → मला).
- ICMR-NIN adults paragraph: "only the point of splitting it into 5 days is extra" → "फरक एवढाच की ते हालचाल 5 दिवसांत विभागायलाही सांगतात" ("the only difference is that they also say to spread the activity over 5 days").
- "Selected: copy it now": आत्ताच ("right now", urgent) → आता.
- IPAQ Moderate/High rules: "a week" now also attached to the MET-minutes ("आठवड्याला किमान 600 / 3000 MET-मिनिटे"), so 600 cannot be read as per day.
- Ordinals written joined, as Marathi does: "3 रा", "70 व्या" → "3रा", "70व्या" (15 strings; numbers unchanged, checker passes).

## Not changed, and why

- "(in English)" notes after links to For Doctors · Tools, the added "(The account pages are in English.)", and links to `/mr/...` pages: by design.
- "Alerts:" shown as सूचना (note): the site uses सूचना for "Alert" everywhere (the Alert label, the doctor-view line); consistent, meaning kept.
- "This page is not in {name} yet: opens the home page" → मुख्य पेज. The back-translator suggested "English page", but the English means that language's home page; मुख्य पेज (home page) is right.
- "5 to 18 years" in the children's BMI FAQ: the English says 18 too.
- BMI article description without "at the same BMI": the English description also has no such qualifier. "Chemically active" in the waist article: the English says "chemically active".
- "downstream diseases" → "त्यातून होणाऱ्या आजारांवर" (the diseases that come from it): the same wording Hindi settled on.
- "review" schedule / marks → उजळणी (revision): what spaced review means in Marathi; the book reader itself is English-only.
- "Severely low sugar" → "खूप कमी शुगर" (very low sugar): still distinct from "कमी शुगर" (low sugar); meaning kept.
- Small label shifts with no meaning change: Coming → लवकरच (soon); Post / Guide and Guides identical in singular and plural; "Gender" shown as लिंग (sex); "Neither" → कोणीही नाही; "Discuss with a doctor" → "understand it by talking to a doctor"; "effect size" glossed in brackets; "Being written" → लिहिणे सुरू आहे.

## Open questions (decisions, not errors)

1. **पट्टा for "range/band"** (healthy range, "you are within this range", "{kg} kg below this range", 9 strings). Understandable next to
   numbers, but its everyday meaning is a belt or strip. Alternatives: रेंज (as people say it) or मर्यादा. Changing it touches 9 strings.
2. **"Reversing obesity"** is now "वाढत्या लठ्ठपणाला मागे फिरवणे" (turning back rising obesity), in line with Hindi.
3. **कृश for "wasted"**: chosen because कुपोषित (malnourished) says more than a low BMI-for-age shows. If a published Marathi WHO
   term is preferred, it can replace कृश in three strings.
4. **Validated questionnaires** (STOP-Bang, IPAQ, IDRS): the Marathi wording is this site's AI translation, not a published validated
   Marathi version (as for Hindi).
