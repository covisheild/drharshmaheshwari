## Not changed, and why

- By design, not findings: links to `/te/` pages; "(అకౌంట్ పేజీలు ఇంగ్లీష్‌లో ఉంటాయి)" and "(in English)" notes on links to English-only pages; English kept inside `<span lang="en">`; references, dates and `draft` taken from the English file.
- "free" (ఉచితంగా) in six strings ("Everything here is free to read and use", "Free BMI ... calculator", "Free health calculators", "Help keep this project free" x2, "intended to remain freely accessible"): the English says free and none is on a book page; the book, dataset and PDF strings do not say free (the licence line keeps "free to copy, share and adapt" as స్వేచ్ఛ, as allowed).
- Minor, left: "Answer the four questions" → "all four" (same meaning); "Coming" → "Soon" (label pair, same as Hindi); "Selected: copy it now" reads "we have selected it" (harmless); AM/PM kept in Latin (widely read; numbers and placeholders untouched); "{people} భారతీయ కట్-ఆఫ్" word order ("men's Indian cut-off") is understandable and changing it needs the placeholder's case ending checked.
- The International Physical Activity Questionnaire keeps its transliterated name (క్వశ్చనేర్), as a proper name.

## Open questions (decisions, not errors)

1. **Vigorous = గట్టి శ్రమ, energetic = జోరైన** (new glossary terms). An alternative for vigorous is "ఎక్కువ శ్రమ (ఊపిరి ఆడనంత)"; గట్టి was chosen because it matches the page's own description ("so hard you cannot talk freely", గట్టిగా).
2. **Sex = లింగం** everywhere (the word on Indian government forms). If a softer word is wanted for parents, "అబ్బాయి/అమ్మాయి" could be used on the children's lines only.
3. **"Reversing obesity" = "పెరుగుతున్న ఊబకాయాన్ని వెనక్కి తిప్పడం"** (turning back rising obesity), the same choice as Hindi.
4. **Wasted = "బాగా సన్నగా ఉండటం"** (very thin) rather than a clinical term; doctors still see the SD threshold beside it.
