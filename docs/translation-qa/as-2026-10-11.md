## Not changed, and why

- "Everything here is free to read and use" (বিনামূলীয়া), "Free BMI ... calculator", "Help keep this project free", "core educational resources ... freely accessible": the English says free about the site as a whole; Hindi keeps मुफ़्त in the same keys. No book, dataset or PDF is called free (the `bannedOnBookPages` check passes).
- "if you have been inactive" (ICMR-NIN advice before exercise): the English says inactive; নিষ্ক্ৰিয় kept.
- BMI article description: "Indians carry more body fat ... than Europeans" without "at the same BMI": the English description has no such qualifier either; the key points and body say "at the same BMI" (একে BMI তে), as in the English.
- "Disclaimer" (দায়বৰ্জন): a glossary term and a heading; formal, but the usual Assamese word. Kept.
- "Everyone" (phone header label): সকলো ("all") is short and reads as the mode with "For Doctors" beside it. Kept.
- Small plural/singular differences that Assamese does not mark (Calculator / Calculators, Post / Posts, "{n} reading", "Full-length books"), "example people" vs "the example person", "very tall and very short" ("বেছি ওখ আৰু বেছি চাপৰ"), "celebration of meritocracy" ("respect for merit"), "Wasted" read as "thin" (ক্ষীণ, with "−2 SD" beside it), "manikin (doll)", "None yet" read as "not yet": meaning kept, no fix needed.
- "(The account pages are in English.)" and "(in English)" after links to English-only pages: by design, as in every language.

## Open questions (decisions, not errors)

1. **Energetic = খৰতকীয়া** ("brisk") with "(moderate to vigorous)" after it. It is an intensity word and clearly differs from vigorous (জোৰদাৰ) and from "active" (সক্ৰিয়), but it is close to "brisk walking" (খৰকৈ খোজ কঢ়া), which is moderate activity. A descriptive alternative would be "উশাহ খৰ হোৱা" ("that makes you breathe faster").
2. **Activity = লৰচৰ** throughout (tool name "শাৰীৰিক লৰচৰ পৰীক্ষা"). The alternative "শাৰীৰিক সক্ৰিয়তা" is more formal.
3. **Disclaimer = দায়বৰ্জন** is understood by educated readers only; a plainer heading would need a longer phrase.
