## Minor findings

Fixed:

- "Reverse obesity" (About description, tagline under the photo, home tagline): "push back obesity" (ಹಿಮ್ಮೆಟ್ಟಿಸು, a military word) → "ಹೆಚ್ಚುತ್ತಿರುವ ಬೊಜ್ಜನ್ನು ಹಿಂದಕ್ಕೆ ತಿರುಗಿಸು" (turn back rising obesity), the Hindi choice.
- STOP-Bang short labels `Observed`, `Pressure` and the list in the scoring help: "the thing noticed", "pressure" → "ಬೇರೆಯವರು ಗಮನಿಸಿದ್ದು" (what others noticed), "ಬಿಪಿ (ಪ್ರೆಶರ್)".
- `No exercise, and sitting (sedentary) activities...`: the gloss ನಿಷ್ಕ್ರಿಯ ("inactive, idle", a label the site avoids) → ಸೆಡೆಂಟರಿ.
- "diseases that come **after** obesity" (2 strings) → ಬೊಜ್ಜಿನಿಂದ ಬರುವ (that come from obesity).
- `Reading the evidence and turning it...`: ಓದಿ read first as "Read!" → ಓದುವುದು ಮತ್ತು...
- `Where do your numbers fall?` ("where do your numbers come [from]") → ಯಾವ ವ್ಯಾಪ್ತಿಯಲ್ಲಿವೆ? (in which range are they?).
- `Latest at the clinic` ("the clinic's latest one") → ಕ್ಲಿನಿಕ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ತೆಗೆದ ಇತ್ತೀಚಿನ ರೀಡಿಂಗ್.
- `The recordings come from a clinical teaching manikin`: "manikin (doll) from", with the ending cut off after the bracket → "a human-shaped teaching model (manikin)".
- Anaesthetist gloss "(anaesthesia)" → "(ಅನಸ್ತೇಶಿಯಾ ಕೊಡುವವರು)" (the one who gives anaesthesia).
- Number placeholders with a case ending cut off after them (8 strings: `Healthy BMI at this age: {lo} to under {hi}`, `...thinness below {p3}, overweight from {ow}, obesity from {ob}`, `{min} of {target} minutes a week` (2), `Keep your waist under {half} cm` (2), `Under {cut} cm`, `under {cm}`). They were grammatical after a numeral, but reworded so every placeholder stands alone ("ಕನಿಷ್ಠ {lo}, ಮತ್ತು ಈ ಮಿತಿಗಿಂತ ಕಡಿಮೆ: {hi}"; "ಗುರಿ: {target} ನಿಮಿಷ"; "ಇದಕ್ಕಿಂತ ಕಡಿಮೆ: {cm}").
- BMI text: heading "Keeping my number, what should I do?" → "ನನ್ನ BMI ಸಂಖ್ಯೆ ತಿಳಿದ ಮೇಲೆ ನಾನು ಏನು ಮಾಡಬೇಕು?"; "two blind places" → "two limits (two things it cannot see)"; "firm muscles" → ಸ್ನಾಯುಬಲ ಹೆಚ್ಚಿರುವ (muscular).
- Waist text: tape "tight" (ಬಿಗಿ) → "fitting the body (not loose)"; heading "what is the **story** of the cut-offs" → "what about the cut-offs"; ಕುಳ್ಳ (short, a little mocking) → ಕಡಿಮೆ ಎತ್ತರದ.

Not changed (minor, meaning kept): "at this sitting" / "{n} sittings" as "this time" / "times" (the averaging rule is stated elsewhere); `Are you a man?` blunt but clear; "Credits" as ಕೃತಜ್ಞತೆ (thanks), usual in Kannada; `How good is it?`, `How long it works`, `How was it measured?` are as vague as the English; "breathless" as "breath feels blocked"; `Maintenance` (ನಿರ್ವಹಣೆ, also "management"; the support page context makes it clear); `Me` as ನಾನು; `Stay in the loop` as "keep getting information"; `Timeline` as ಕಾಲಾನುಕ್ರಮ (bookish); `(optional)` once as ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ and elsewhere ಕಡ್ಡಾಯವಲ್ಲ; ಪೃಷ್ಠ (buttocks, formal) for hips; BMI title "Is BMI the right measure" for "accurate"; the colloquial example "missed my tablet"; `Known diabetes?` without a subject; IDRS "first sieve"; transliterated "obstructive sleep apnoea" and statistics words (glossed nearby); the blog post's "experience it differently" (feel), "some control" (influence), "must be recognised" (worth recognising), all in an essay's register.

## Not findings (by design or as in the English)

- Links to `/kn/...` pages, "(ಇಂಗ್ಲಿಷ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ)" / "(The account pages are in English.)" notes on links to English-only pages, English kept inside `<span lang="en">` (the citation), placeholders; {kids} and {people} are filled with genitive words (ಹುಡುಗರ, ಗಂಡಸರ), so "{kids} ಪೈಕಿ", "{people} ಭಾರತೀಯ ಕಟ್-ಆಫ್" are correct.
- `{n}st/nd/rd/th` → `{n}ನೇ`: the ordinal suffix is the same for every number in Kannada.
- "Free" (ಉಚಿತ) on the calculators and the support lines: the English says free and these are not book pages.
- Children's BMI help (`Children are still growing...`) says 5 to 18 years because **the English says "From 5 to 18 years"** (see Open questions).
- BMI text description "Indians ... develop diabetes at a lower BMI" and the waist text "one reason diabetes comes early in India": the English is just as direct; where the English hedges ("may have", "risk", "more likely") the Kannada does too.
- ಬೊಜ್ಜು for obesity: kept (see Open questions).

## Open questions (decisions, not errors)

1. **ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಯ ಸುತ್ತಳತೆ for waist.** It is unmistakable (the tape round the belly) but longer than ಸೊಂಟ. Check that the field labels ("2. ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಯ ಸುತ್ತಳತೆ", "ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಯ ಸುತ್ತಳತೆ, ಗಂಡಸರು"), the calculator picture on the home page and the ratio row fit at 390 px. "ಸೊಂಟದ ಮೂಳೆ" is still used for the hip bone, because that is what the bone is called; the waist itself is never called ಸೊಂಟ.
2. **ಬೊಜ್ಜು for obesity** is kept. It is the word Kannada families and health writing use, though in speech it can also mean "flab" ("ಹೊಟ್ಟೆಯ ಬೊಜ್ಜು" = belly flab). The medical alternative ಸ್ಥೂಲಕಾಯ is Sanskritised and outside the glossary's register. Changing it would touch about 60 strings; say if you want it.
3. **5 to 18 or 5 to 17?** The English help text `Children are still growing...` says "From 5 to 18 years" for the IAP charts, while the calculator and every other line say 5 to 17. The Kannada follows the English. If the English is changed, every language follows.
4. **ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಶ್ರಮದ for vigorous** ("high-effort") is clear and distinct from ಮಧ್ಯಮ (moderate) and from ತೀವ್ರ (severe), but as a bare option label (`Vigorous` in the IDRS activity choices) it is an adjective without a noun, as the English is.
5. **Disclaimer** is now "ಸೂಚನೆ (ಡಿಸ್‌ಕ್ಲೈಮರ್)" (notice, with the English word in brackets) rather than the legal calque. If a shorter footer link is needed, "ಸೂಚನೆ" alone would do.

### Voice notes (from the rewrite's style sheet)

- Reader: a family in Bengaluru, Mysuru or Hubballi; writer: their family doctor writing a popular health column. Warm, plain, never stiff.
- Polite ನೀವು with -ಇ imperatives (ಅಳೆಯಿರಿ, ಹಾಕಿ, ನೋಡಿ); wishes with -ಲಿ ("... ಕಡಿಮೆ ಇರಲಿ").
- Spoken-written register: ಯಾಕೆ over ಏಕೆ, ಅಂದರೆ over ಎಂದರೆ, ಸಲ over ಬಾರಿ, ಹೊತ್ತು for "how long", ಮಾತಾಡು, ಓಡಾಡು, ಕಳಿಸು.
- Emphasis by particles (-ಏ, -ಊ, ಅಷ್ಟೇ, ಅಂತಲ್ಲ), not extra words; active personal verbs over passives; privacy lines "ಎಲ್ಲಿಗೂ ಹೋಗುವುದಿಲ್ಲ, ಎಲ್ಲೂ ಉಳಿಯುವುದೂ ಇಲ್ಲ".
- Questions as people ask them ("ನನ್ನ ತೂಕ ಎಷ್ಟಕ್ಕೆ ಬರಬೇಕು?", "ಇದನ್ನು ಎಷ್ಟು ನಂಬಬಹುದು?").
- UI words: ಐಚ್ಛಿಕ, ಉಳಿಸಿ, ಆವೃತ್ತಿ, ಸದ್ಯದಲ್ಲೇ, ರೆಡಿ, ಆಫ್‌ಲೈನ್ ಇದ್ದೀರಿ, ಇಂಟರ್ನೆಟ್ ಸಿಕ್ಕಾಗ / ಮತ್ತೆ ಬಂದಾಗ, ಅರ್ಧಂಬರ್ಧ ಉಳಿಸಲಾಗಿದೆ; everyday loanwords in Kannada script (ರೀಡಿಂಗ್, ಲಿಂಕ್, ಸ್ಲೀಪ್ ಸ್ಟಡಿ, ಟಮ್ಮಿ ಟೈಮ್); glossary health words unchanged.
- Activity: brisk walking ಬಿರುಸಿನ ನಡಿಗೆ; energetic ಚುರುಕಾದ (ಮಧ್ಯಮದಿಂದ ಹೆಚ್ಚು ಶ್ರಮದ); mild ಹಗುರ; strenuous manual work ಶ್ರಮದ ಕೈಕೆಲಸ; active play ಓಡಿಯಾಡುವ ಆಟ; infant "active" ಕೈಕಾಲು ಆಡಿಸುವುದು; crawling ಅಂಬೆಗಾಲು; spread through the day ದಿನವಿಡೀ ಬಿಡಿಬಿಡಿಯಾಗಿ.
- Alerts are short statements; first aid uses ಟೀ ಚಮಚ and "ಬೇಗ ರಕ್ತಕ್ಕೆ ಸೇರುವ ಸಕ್ಕರೆ"; drowsy = ತೂಕಡಿಸು, confused = ಗೊಂದಲ, no intensifier.

- Blog: first person, reflective, short clauses joined by semicolons; "ಅರ್ಥಮಾಡಿಕೊಳ್ಳುವ ರೀತಿ", not ಅರ್ಥೈಸುವಿಕೆ.
