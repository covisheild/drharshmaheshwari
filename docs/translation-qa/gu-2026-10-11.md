# Gujarati QA, 11 Oct 2026 (meaning comparison, pipeline step 4b)

Checked: the whole dictionary `src/i18n/gu.json` (1095 strings) and the three texts
(`learn/bmi-cut-offs-for-indians`, `learn/waist-to-height-ratio`, `blog/interpretation-of-the-world`), front matter included.
A blind back-translation (made by an agent that saw only the Gujarati) was compared pair by pair with the English: a script printed
all 1095 pairs in batches and each was read, then every line of the three texts. The back-translator's notes on unnatural or
ambiguous Gujarati were checked against the English and graded with the rest. Register: everyday Gujarati for a family in Ahmedabad
or Surat (`src/i18n/glossary/gu.json`). Steps as in `docs/translation.md`.

## Minor findings

Fixed:

- `(partly saved)`, `Partly saved ({percent}%)`: "a little got saved" (થોડું) → અધૂરું સેવ થયું (incompletely saved).
- `Being written` (the Books card when there are no books yet): singular "it is being written" → લખાઈ રહ્યાં છે (they are being written).
- `Apr`, `Jul`: truncated એપ્રિ, જુલા looked like typos → એપ્રિલ, જુલાઈ in full (short enough).
- Book reader privacy lines: "study marks" (અભ્યાસના માર્ક, like exam marks) → પ્રેક્ટિસના માર્ક (practice marks).
- `swimming laps`: "swimming length upon length" → સ્વિમિંગ પૂલમાં અટક્યા વગર તરવું (swimming in a pool without stopping).
- IPAQ note: "they are not **like** the targets above" (vague) → ઉપરનાં લક્ષ્યોથી અલગ છે (different from the targets above).
- IPAQ FAQ: "divides into low, moderate or high-in" (case ending after quoted words) → ત્રણ શ્રેણીમાં વહેંચે છે: નીચું, મધ્યમ કે ઊંચું.
- `vigorous minutes count double`, `15 min vigorous` in the adult target: જોરદાર followed by પ્રવૃત્તિ, as in finding 4.
- Waist text: "the ratio **adds a correction** for height" (calque) → ગુણોત્તર ઊંચાઈને પણ ધ્યાનમાં લે છે (also takes height into account).
- Blog: "our **instant** surroundings" (તરતની, time not place) → આપણા નજીકના વાતાવરણમાં (in our immediate surroundings).
- Blog: previous generations (આગલી, correct but the word reads both ways, finding 2) → અગાઉની પેઢીઓ.

Not changed (minor, meaning kept): `About` → પરિચય ("Introduction", usual for a page name); singular and plural alike in Gujarati (`Guide`/`Guides`, `Days`, `{n} sitting`, `{n} day`, "the first guide(s)/post(s) are being written"); "downstream / follow obesity" → "caused by / that come after obesity" (four lines; within the site's own framing); `Alert` → ચેતવણી ("warning", the usual word, no added alarm); "updates in the background" → "updates itself later" (two app lines); `Wasted`, `Severely wasted` → "thin" (the SD beside them tells them apart); `Reflections` (blog topic) → વિચારો ("thoughts"); `Learn` label; `High` (ઊંચું, also "tall"; always beside a risk or IPAQ word); "fads" → ફૅશનેબલ નુસખા ("fashionable tricks"); "Adam's apple" → હૈડિયો (correct Gujarati, a bit old-fashioned); "make muscles work hard" stiff (two lines); the waist text title "**a** number worth checking" for "the one number" (also the link text in the BMI text); `Cover of {title}` → મુખપૃષ્ઠ; home tagline "one clear thought after another"; blog: "very hard or close to impossible" → "very hard, almost impossible", stacked possessives in section 2; `As for 20–60` terse (a table cell); `Main` (navigation name).

## Not findings (by design or as in the English)

- Links to `/gu/...` pages, "(અંગ્રેજીમાં)" / "(The account pages are in English.)" notes on links to English-only pages, English kept inside `<span lang="en">` (the citation, browser menu names), placeholders.
- `{n}st/nd/rd/th` → `{n}`: the templates say "percentile {ordinal}".
- IDRS bands "30 to 50 moderate, 60 or more high, below 30 low": the English says the same, and IDRS scores move in steps of 10, so there is no score between 50 and 60.
- "Free" (મફત) on the calculators, the home line and the support lines: the English says free and these are not book pages (the book pages are checked by `bannedOnBookPages`).
- Hip / hip bone (થાપાનું હાડકું), teaspoons (નાની ચમચી (ટીસ્પૂન)), confusion (મૂંઝવણભરી સ્થિતિ, read as "not understanding what is going on"), strapped in (belt fastened), AM/PM, adult 18+ (પુખ્ત), contribution (યોગદાન): all came through as meant.

## Open questions (decisions, not errors)

- Sex label: `લિંગ (પુરુષ/સ્ત્રી)` was chosen over a bare લિંગ (formal, and in some contexts the anatomical word) or a bare "પુરુષ/સ્ત્રી". Harsh or a Gujarati reader may prefer one of the others.
- `Reflections` (the blog topic) stays વિચારો ("thoughts"); ચિંતન would be closer to "reflections" but more literary. It must match the blog post's topic, so change both together if wanted.
- The waist text's title ("a number worth checking") could say "the one number" more strongly (એકમાત્ર આંકડો), but that reads as "the only number", which the BMI text contradicts.
