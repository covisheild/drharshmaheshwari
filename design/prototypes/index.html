Fonts are new in all three. Signal uses Sora, Geist and Geist Mono with dot-matrix numerals. Atlas uses Archivo (a wide grotesque) with JetBrains Mono. Beam uses Outfit, DM Sans and DM Mono. The round photo of you is kept as it is. All three animate: items rise in as you scroll, numbers count up, and the heart-sound playhead moves. Animation switches off for visitors who prefer reduced motion. Pages are static HTML and CSS with a few lines of script, so the real site would stay fast.
